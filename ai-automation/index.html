You are on a roof or behind the counter. An enquiry comes in. By the time you reply, the customer may have already called someone else.

Automation handles the first reply, the follow-up and the booking, so you do not have to do it by hand.

Questions

Things you are probably wondering.

What can a small business automate?

Common examples are instant replies to new enquiries, follow-up emails and SMS, booking confirmations and reminders, and CRM updates. Exactly what we automate depends on your business.

What is a CRM?

A CRM is a system that keeps track of your customers and enquiries in one place, so nothing gets lost. We set it up and connect it to your website and follow-up.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We set it up, explain it in plain English and show you what you control.

Will automation make me sound like a robot?

Not if it is done properly. We write replies that sound like you, and a real person stays in charge of the conversations that matter.

Can you automate my existing business?

Often, yes. The repetitive stuff that eats your evenings is usually a good place to start. We tell you honestly if it is worth it.

Do I need a long contract?

No. We would rather keep you because it is working than because a contract says so. We are upfront about terms before anything starts.
