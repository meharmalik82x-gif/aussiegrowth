Why Google Ads

Be there when they search.

Google Ads shows your business to people at the moment they search, for example "plumber near me" or "roof repairs". People who search are usually ready to act.

That is why Google Ads suits tradies, home-service businesses and local operators who need the phone to ring.

The landing page

Clicks are easy. Calls are the point.

A click costs money whether or not the visitor calls. So we send ad traffic to a page built to turn visitors into enquiries: a clear service, a clear next step, and a call button or quote form that works on a phone.

If you do not have that page yet, we build it.

Questions

Things you are probably wondering.

What is Google Ads management?

It is the setup and ongoing running of your Google ads: choosing what to target, writing the ads, tracking results and improving the campaign over time.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. We track enquiries, calls and bookings, tell you what we see, and keep improving the campaign.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your industry, your area and how many jobs you can take on. We give you a realistic starting budget, not a made-up number designed to sound impressive.

Do I need a website first?

You need a page that turns clicks into calls. If your site does not do that, we can build one, including a $0 website for two Australian businesses every Monday.

Can you run Google Ads and Facebook ads together?

Yes. Google catches people who are searching now. Meta ads reach people before they search. Many businesses use both.

Do I need a long contract?

No. We would rather keep you because it is working than because a contract says so. We are upfront about terms before anything starts.
