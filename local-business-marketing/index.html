What a good local site has

The short checklist.

On the site

  • What you do, in plain language
  • Where you are and who you serve
  • Real photos and real projects
  • A call button and a quote or booking form
  • Fast and easy on a phone

Behind the site

  • Ads that reach people in your area
  • Tracking for calls and enquiries
  • Instant replies to new enquiries
  • Follow-up so leads do not go cold

Questions

Things you are probably wondering.

What kinds of local businesses do you work with?

Clinics, gyms, hospitality, automotive, retail and professional services, as well as tradies and home-service businesses.

What should a local business website include?

A clear description of what you do, where you are, real photos or projects, a call button, a quote or booking form, and a site that loads fast and works well on a phone.

Do you run ads for local businesses?

Yes. We run Google, Meta and Instagram ads aimed at people in your area, and we track the enquiries they bring in.

Can you set up automatic replies and bookings?

Yes. We set up instant replies, follow-up by email and SMS, and booking automation, so new enquiries get an answer straight away.

Do I need a long contract?

No. We would rather keep you because it is working than because a contract says so. We are upfront about terms before anything starts.
